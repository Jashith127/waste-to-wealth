## What to build

2. **Chickens:** a few white broilers as simple chunky shapes (art bible §2 exaggeration). A flock of about ten is enough. Not cute and not cartoonish: the same 2:1 miniature look.
3. **Stall:** the S1 frontage under the midday preset, a tray of chicken on the counter, Riyaz idle. No kilo.
4. **Between them:** a strip of road, or the cut edge of two plinths, so the two places read as separate (art bible §6.1).
